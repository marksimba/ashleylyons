The Artist

Just getting started.

Ashley Lyons is a singer-songwriter writing music to share her experiences, testimony, and love for her Savior, Jesus Christ. Her debut single, He Already Did, is her first release — a song about trusting that the Lord can do His mighty work.

This is the beginning. New songs are on the way, and the heart behind them is simple: to sing to the Lord as long as she lives.
